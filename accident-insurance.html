How it works

What is accident insurance?

Accident insurance is supplemental coverage designed to provide fixed cash benefits when certain covered accidental injuries occur.

It does not replace major medical health insurance. Instead, it can provide additional financial protection when an accident leads to medical treatment or other unexpected expenses.

Benefit amounts and covered services are determined by the terms and benefit schedule of the policy.

Potential benefits

What can accident insurance help cover?

Covered events and benefit amounts vary by policy, but accident insurance may provide benefits for a range of accident-related services.

Emergency Care

Some policies may provide benefits for covered emergency room visits or urgent treatment after an accident.

Fractures & Dislocations

Covered fractures or dislocations may qualify for fixed benefits based on the policy's benefit schedule.

Ambulance Services

Certain plans may provide benefits for covered ground or air ambulance transportation.

Diagnostic Testing

Benefits may be available for certain covered diagnostic services related to an accidental injury.

Hospital Care

Some accident policies may include benefits for covered hospital treatment following an accident.

Follow-Up Treatment

Depending on the policy, benefits may be available for certain follow-up visits, therapy, or other covered services.

Who may consider it

Who might consider accident insurance?

Accident insurance may be worth considering if an unexpected injury could create a meaningful financial burden for you or your household.

  • You have a higher health insurance deductible
  • You have children involved in sports or active hobbies
  • You participate in recreational activities
  • You want additional protection for unexpected injuries
  • You want coverage that supplements your health insurance

Important details

What should you review before choosing a policy?

Accident insurance policies can vary. Before choosing coverage, review when benefits are payable, how much the policy pays, and what exclusions or limitations may apply.

  • Covered injuries and services
  • Benefit amounts
  • Benefit limits
  • Exclusions and limitations
  • Waiting periods, when applicable
  • Monthly premium

Frequently asked questions

Accident Insurance FAQs

Is accident insurance the same as health insurance?

No. Accident insurance is supplemental coverage and is not a replacement for comprehensive major medical health insurance.

How are accident insurance benefits paid?

Benefits are generally paid according to the fixed amounts and terms listed in the policy when a covered accidental injury occurs.

Does accident insurance only cover hospital stays?

Not necessarily. Depending on the policy, covered benefits may include emergency treatment, fractures, ambulance services, diagnostic testing, hospital care, and other accident-related services.

Can accident insurance work with my health insurance?

Accident insurance may be purchased as supplemental coverage alongside major medical insurance, subject to carrier, state, eligibility, and policy requirements.

Can accident benefits help with expenses outside of medical bills?

Depending on the policy structure, benefits may be paid directly to the insured and may help with a variety of expenses.
