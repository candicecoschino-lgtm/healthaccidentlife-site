How it works

What is hospital indemnity insurance?

Hospital indemnity insurance is supplemental coverage designed to provide fixed cash benefits when certain covered hospital events occur.

It does not replace major medical health insurance. Benefits are generally paid according to the terms and benefit schedule of the policy rather than the total cost of your medical care.

These benefits may provide additional financial protection when a covered hospital stay creates expenses beyond what your health insurance pays.

Potential benefits

What can hospital indemnity insurance help with?

Benefits vary by policy, but hospital indemnity coverage may provide fixed cash benefits for certain covered hospital events.

Hospital Admission

Some policies provide a fixed benefit when you are admitted to a hospital for a covered condition.

Hospital Confinement

Certain plans may provide benefits for covered days spent in the hospital.

Intensive Care

Some plans may provide additional benefits for covered intensive care stays.

Medical Cost Sharing

Cash benefits may help with expenses such as deductibles, copays, coinsurance, or other healthcare-related costs.

Other Expenses

Depending on the policy, benefits paid directly to the insured may help with expenses that arise during a covered hospital stay.

Added Protection

Hospital indemnity insurance is designed to supplement existing health coverage, not replace major medical insurance.

Who may consider it

Who might consider hospital indemnity insurance?

Hospital indemnity coverage may be worth considering if a hospital stay could create additional financial pressure for your household.

  • You have a higher health insurance deductible
  • You want additional protection for hospital-related expenses
  • You want fixed benefits for certain covered hospital events
  • You want additional coverage alongside your health insurance
  • You want benefits that may help with expenses during a hospital stay

Important details

What should you review before choosing a policy?

Hospital indemnity policies can vary. Before choosing coverage, review when benefits are payable, how much the policy pays, and what exclusions or limitations may apply.

  • Hospital admission benefit amounts
  • Daily confinement benefits
  • Intensive care benefits
  • Benefit limits
  • Waiting periods, when applicable
  • Exclusions and limitations
  • Pre-existing condition provisions, when applicable
  • Monthly premium

Frequently asked questions

Hospital Indemnity Insurance FAQs

Is hospital indemnity insurance the same as health insurance?

No. Hospital indemnity insurance is supplemental coverage and is not a replacement for comprehensive major medical health insurance.

How are hospital indemnity benefits paid?

Benefits are generally paid according to the fixed benefit amounts and terms listed in the policy when a covered event occurs.

Can hospital indemnity benefits help with expenses outside the hospital?

Depending on the policy structure, benefits may be paid directly to the insured and may help with a variety of expenses. Policy terms determine when benefits are payable.

Does hospital indemnity insurance have a deductible?

Policy designs vary. Hospital indemnity insurance works differently from major medical coverage, so the specific benefit structure and policy terms should be reviewed before enrolling.

Can hospital indemnity insurance work with my health insurance?

Hospital indemnity insurance may be purchased as supplemental coverage alongside major medical insurance, subject to carrier, state, eligibility, and policy requirements.

What's the difference between hospital indemnity and accident insurance?

Hospital indemnity insurance generally focuses on covered hospital events, while accident insurance generally provides benefits for certain covered accidental injuries and related services. Coverage and benefits vary by policy.
