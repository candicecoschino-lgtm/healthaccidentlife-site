Hospital indemnity insurance for the costs health insurance may not cover.

How it works

What is hospital indemnity insurance?

Hospital indemnity insurance is a supplemental insurance product designed to provide fixed cash benefits when certain covered hospital events occur.

Unlike major medical insurance, hospital indemnity coverage generally does not pay your healthcare provider directly based on the full cost of medical treatment. Instead, covered benefits are typically paid according to the terms of the policy.

Those benefits can help with expenses that may still exist even when you already have health insurance.

Potential benefits

What can hospital indemnity benefits help with?

Benefits depend on the policy, but hospital indemnity coverage may provide cash payments for certain covered events.

Hospital Admission

Some policies provide a fixed benefit when you are admitted to a hospital for a covered condition.

Hospital Confinement

Certain plans may provide benefits for covered days spent hospitalized.

Intensive Care

Some plans may provide additional benefits for covered intensive care stays.

Medical Cost Sharing

Cash benefits may help offset deductibles, copays, coinsurance, or other healthcare-related expenses.

Household Expenses

Because benefits are generally paid as cash according to the policy terms, they may help with other expenses during a covered hospital event.

Added Protection

Hospital indemnity insurance is designed to supplement existing health coverage, not replace major medical insurance.

Who may consider it

Who might benefit from hospital indemnity coverage?

Hospital indemnity insurance may be worth considering if a hospital stay could create a meaningful financial burden for your household.

  • You have a higher health insurance deductible
  • You want additional protection against hospital-related expenses
  • Your household relies heavily on your income
  • You have limited emergency savings
  • You want cash benefits that may help with non-medical expenses
  • You want to supplement your existing major medical coverage

Important details

What should you review before choosing a policy?

Hospital indemnity policies can vary significantly. Before enrolling, it is important to understand exactly when benefits are payable and what limitations apply.

  • Hospital admission benefit amounts
  • Daily confinement benefits
  • Intensive care benefits
  • Benefit limits
  • Waiting periods, when applicable
  • Exclusions and limitations
  • Pre-existing condition provisions, when applicable
  • Monthly premium

Frequently asked questions

Hospital Indemnity Insurance FAQs

Is hospital indemnity insurance the same as health insurance?

No. Hospital indemnity insurance is supplemental coverage and is not a replacement for major medical health insurance.

How are hospital indemnity benefits paid?

Benefits are generally paid according to the fixed benefit amounts and terms listed in the policy when a covered event occurs.

Can I use the benefit for expenses outside the hospital?

Depending on the policy structure, benefits may be paid directly to the insured and can potentially help with various expenses. Policy terms determine how and when benefits are payable.

Does hospital indemnity insurance have a deductible?

Policy designs vary. Hospital indemnity insurance generally works differently from major medical coverage, so it is important to review the specific benefit structure and policy terms.

Can hospital indemnity coverage work with my ACA health plan?

Hospital indemnity insurance may be purchased as supplemental coverage alongside major medical insurance, subject to carrier, state, eligibility, and policy requirements.
