Life insurance basics

What is life insurance?

Life insurance is designed to provide a financial benefit to the beneficiaries named in the policy when the insured person dies, subject to the terms of the policy.

Life insurance may help provide money for expenses such as housing, debt, education, final expenses, or the loss of household income.

The type and amount of coverage to consider depends on your financial responsibilities, goals, budget, and how long you want the protection to last.

Coverage types

What types of life insurance are available?

Life insurance generally falls into two broad categories: term life insurance and permanent life insurance.

Term Life Insurance

Term life insurance provides coverage for a specified period, such as 10, 20, or 30 years, subject to the policy terms.

Permanent Life Insurance

Permanent life insurance is designed to provide longer-term coverage and may include cash value features, depending on the type of policy.

Coverage Amount

The death benefit is the amount payable according to the policy terms and can be selected based on your coverage goals.

Policy Duration

Some people need protection for a specific period, while others may want coverage designed to remain in place longer.

Premium

Premiums can vary based on factors such as age, health, coverage amount, policy type, and underwriting.

Beneficiaries

Beneficiaries are the people or entities designated to receive the policy benefit, subject to the terms of the policy.

How much coverage?

How much life insurance might you need?

There is no single coverage amount that works for everyone. Consider the financial responsibilities and goals you want your life insurance policy to help address.

  • Household income that may need to be replaced
  • Mortgage or rent obligations
  • Outstanding debt
  • Children or other dependents
  • Future education costs
  • Final expenses
  • Existing savings and life insurance
  • Long-term financial goals

Choosing coverage

What should you compare in a life insurance policy?

Life insurance policies can differ in cost, duration, features, underwriting requirements, and how premiums work. Understanding those differences can help you compare your options.

  • Coverage amount
  • Policy duration
  • Premium amount
  • Premium guarantees
  • Policy exclusions and limitations
  • Available riders
  • Conversion options, when applicable
  • Underwriting requirements

Frequently asked questions

Life Insurance FAQs

What is term life insurance?

Term life insurance provides coverage for a specified period of time, subject to the terms of the policy.

What is permanent life insurance?

Permanent life insurance is designed to provide longer-term coverage and may include cash value features, depending on the policy type.

How much life insurance should I have?

The amount to consider depends on your financial responsibilities, household needs, existing assets, budget, and the goals you want the policy to help address.

Does age affect the cost of life insurance?

Yes. Age is one of several factors that may affect life insurance premiums. Health, coverage amount, policy type, and underwriting can also affect cost.

Do I need a medical exam for life insurance?

Not always. Underwriting requirements vary by carrier and policy. Some life insurance products may require a medical exam or other medical underwriting, while others use different application and eligibility processes.

Can I have more than one life insurance policy?

It may be possible to have more than one life insurance policy, subject to carrier underwriting requirements and the amount of coverage for which you qualify.
